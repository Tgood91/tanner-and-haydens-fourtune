Read before you walk them

  • 312 is two knots, not a city. Five locations sit within 50 units of each other, and three more sit 400 units northeast. The spawn point and the Sheriff’s Office are 4 units apart, so a new player lands practically inside the station.
  • 404 sprawls. Its pins span 1,900 units east to west, and the Del Perro Pier spawn alone is 1,340 units from its nearest neighbour. Anyone spawning there needs a car before anything else.
  • 404 has no jobs. Every job station in the code — sheriff, EMS, mechanic, taxi — is in 312 only. Work in 404 means driving north.
  • Each city now has two hospitals. Going down sends you to whichever one is closer to where you dropped, so the corner of the city you fall in decides where you wake up.
  • The jail is off both plates. Its cell sits at 1691, 2565, east and north of everything mapped here, inside the 404 band. Arrests send you across the channel: an arrest in 404 releases you in 312.
  • The middle band is still unclaimed. The channel on this map covers the stretch the code hands to a third city, so the bridge is a proposal, not a fact yet.
  • Only six pins have been walked. Everything marked placeholder was picked near a stock landmark and has never been stood on in-game.
